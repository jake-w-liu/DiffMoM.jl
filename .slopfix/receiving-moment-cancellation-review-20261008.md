# Receiving moment cancellation and working precision

The independent public Maxwell-transfer and exact axial-integral witnesses
reproduce lost zero-mean via fields on both Julia versions. At the machine
epsilon boundary, a finite field became zero. Near the former1e-5 window,
subtracting rounded hyperbolic values damaged the surviving field. The
fixed cubic also lost the working-type BigFloat approximation contract.

Axial integrals now use their convergent unit-disk series. Colocated
uniform/tapered weights contract the exact series coefficients before
the moments round; coefficient and sine binary scaling preserves range.
The constants follow the field integrals and factorial term ratios. No
new angle cutoff, precision, tolerance, retry or deadline is selected.

Actual both-version public96 witnesses and independent original40-point
quadrature pass the unchanged5e-11 field gate. All original471 radiation
and resource assertions pass both; warmed allocations and inferred
returns match main on every96 witness. Actual derivative and caller-type
checks are separately scoped. The durable test preserves the original
physical fixtures, adds the former cutoff as a historical regression
witness, and uses Julia's working-type approximation contract for
BigFloat. Failed reference/setup attempts are preserved.

Other numerical-policy items, including the existing real(x)>20 receiving
representation switch, remain separately open. Native-reference, full
package, documentation, exact accounting, static gates and own hosted CI
remain required before this candidate is published. No global correctness
or performance optimality claim is made.
