# Public accepted-power product range

Finite Kurokawa waves were rejected when a complex dot overflowed before
cancellation. A passive reactive excitation has exactly zero physical power
but produced a nonfinite stored dot on both supported Julia versions.

Ordinary dot arithmetic remains unchanged. Nonfinite power recovers with an
exact Float64 product sum before taking its physical half and narrowing.
Working precision derives from twice the full Float64 lattice/exponent span
and the binary term count. Four owned MPFR scalar roles are charged against
the existing caller payload budget before allocation. The existing preflight
payload is reused; warmed ordinary calls remain 592 bytes on both versions.

Independent rational references and 58 new tests pass on both versions.
The 2215-assertion focused package checks and 114 unchanged public wave,
project, MNA and native FLOAT integration checks also pass on both versions.
Original accuracy requirements, fixtures, CI and duplication limits remain.
Rare corrected calls allocate 3160 bytes; this measures total allocation for
one warmed call and does not establish allocator peak or universal efficiency.
